How try-on works

Three steps

  1. Add a photo. Full-body, standing, facing the camera. It's saved just for you and reused for every try-on.
  2. Search and pick a piece. Results come live from the stores, with the garment-only picture used where a store has one.
  3. Press “Try it on”. An AI puts that garment on your photo, keeping your face, pose and background. Results collect in “Fits you've tried”.
